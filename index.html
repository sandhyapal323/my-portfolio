About

The short version.

I'm a graphic designer based in Mumbai with two years of professional experience across commercial product and e-commerce design, image editing and retouching, packaging and catalogue layout, and festive/creative design work.

My strongest body of work sits in product and e-commerce creative — image editing, retouching and feature-led advertisements — built alongside hands-on experience in catalogue layout, structural packaging, and promotional and festive illustration. It's a working combination of production-focused Photoshop and Illustrator craft with broader layout and design ability.
